Our Story

Crafted for Kot Chutta

Nestled on the Bypass at 33 Morr, The Pizza Hut brings the art of wood-fired pizza to the heart of Kot Chutta. We use fresh ingredients and time-honoured techniques to create pizzas that are crispy, loaded, and made just for you.

Whether it's a quick lunch or a family feast, our doors are always open. Dine in or call ahead — we deliver hot, fast, and delicious.
